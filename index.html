About CSFDG

Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide resources and skills training for circular product development to entrepreneurs of Caribbean SIDS while raising awareness of vulnerabilities to climate change.

Vision

To develop a thriving circular economy of entrepreneurs and influence effective climate change resilience strategies on Caribbean SIDS.

About CSFDG Programs

CSFDG delivers educational and skills training programs to entrepreneurs within the creative economy of Grenada and CARICOM. Starting with the Kaylia Couturier Program.

Program image
Flagship Initiative · 2025

Kaylia Couturier Program

Precision. Patience. Quality.

The Kaylia Couturier Program, designed by The Kaylia Group, is CSFDG's flagship initiative — teaching high-quality sewing and pattern-making skills, particularly to women. The program begins in Grenada, chosen due to the personal connection of the organization's founder. This initiative will help break down social and cultural barriers on the island, creating opportunities for local talent to gain exposure and develop their skills.

Story

Why Now?

Nestled in the embrace of the Caribbean, Grenada is classified as a Small Island Developing State (SIDS) by the United Nations. Its beauty is intertwined with the vulnerability to climate change. Rising sea levels, intensified hurricanes, and shifting weather patterns pose real threats.

At CSFDG, we believe in harnessing the power of the creative economy to shape a different story. Through art, architecture, fashion, and overall sustainable design, we weave a narrative of awareness and resilience, nurturing a community that not only adapts to climate challenges but actively advocates for change.

Join us on this journey where creativity becomes a catalyst for building climate resilience in the heart of the Caribbean.
